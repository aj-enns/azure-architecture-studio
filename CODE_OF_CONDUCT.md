# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a collaborative environment where people can contribute
regardless of experience, identity, background, or employer. Be respectful,
assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and make room for
others to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and
sharing another person's confidential information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the
community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith.
